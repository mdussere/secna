Where it stops
Three limits, to be honest.
1
A map of volume, not an alarm.
A rare but serious case can drown in a big cluster. Keep a separate watch for critical signals.
2
Small or uniform corpus: nothing to find.
Let the LLM read all of it.
3
The names are a model's proposal.
